FAQ

Questions brands ask us

Do our dentists have to pay anything?

No. Dentists use the portal free. Your brand pays one monthly fee.

Will dentists see your name anywhere?

No. The portal carries your brand name, logo and colours. On the Growth plan it also runs on your own domain, such as portal.yourbrand.com.

Which files can dentists upload?

Intraoral scans (STL, PLY, OBJ), photos, X-rays and PDFs. Large uploads resume automatically if the connection drops.

Can we set our own packages and prices?

Yes. You choose your packages, what each one includes, the price and the currency. Dentists only see your options.

How do dentists pay us?

An invoice is created when a dentist approves a treatment plan. They pay online, and the money goes into your own payment account, not ours.

Can we move our existing cases and dentists?

Yes. Setup and onboarding includes importing your dentist list and open cases, so you can switch without starting from zero.
