      Small aligner brands still run cases through inboxes

      Large aligner companies have their own doctor portals. Most growing brands still take cases by email and WhatsApp, track status in spreadsheets and chase invoices by hand.

      • Missing records. Scans arrive without photos, X-rays or a completed prescription.
      • No single status. Dentists message to ask where their case is.
      • Slow approvals. Treatment plans wait days for a reply.
      • Unpaid invoices. Finance teams chase payments one dentist at a time.

      How it works

      Launch your portal

      Your logo, colours and domain. Set your packages, prices and currency, then invite your dentists.

      Dentists submit cases

      A guided form with autosave, scan uploads and a checklist, so every case arrives complete.

      Plan, approve, produce

      Upload the treatment plan. Dentists approve in one click and follow each status change.

      Get paid

      Invoices are created on approval. Dentists pay online into your own payment account.
